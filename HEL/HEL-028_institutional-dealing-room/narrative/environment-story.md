# The Institutional Dealing Room

A private dealing room with low-reflection instrument walls, execution desks, order rails, exposure wells, compliance glass, and settlement corridors.

This environment exists to support execution, order management, portfolio operations, trade lifecycle, exposure control, and post-trade discipline.

Its governing psychology is silence, precision, restraint, accountability, execution quality.

Signature law: Nothing celebrates movement; the room rewards clean execution, controlled exposure, and complete records.
