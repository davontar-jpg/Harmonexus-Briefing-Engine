HARMONEXUS · MARKET ENGINE

Market intelligence, with evidence.

The Harmonexus Briefing Engine is an owner-operated market research and decision-support application. It combines economic observations, futures positioning, authenticated market observations and historical research into instrument readings, source audits and morning briefings.

Open the existing Market Engine dashboard

Google access

The owner authorizes Google Sheets and Apps Script to read and update the configured market workbook, maintain scheduled jobs and send briefings to configured recipients. Deployment tooling also accesses authorized project and file metadata. The dashboard can use a service account shared onto the workbook.

This application provides analytical context. It does not execute trades. Observations can be delayed, stale, incomplete or unavailable; historical relationships do not guarantee future outcomes.
