# HEL-035 Operator Guide

## Start with the market state

Read the Silver Market Desk from top to bottom:

1. **Executive Market Assessment** — the assembled silver operating state;
2. **Monetary Environment** — dollar, yield, curve, positioning, and
   participation pressure;
3. **VIX — Volatility Environment** — risk appetite, expected range, and
   liquidation conditions;
4. **Liquidity Environment** — whether COMEX order-book interpretation is
   authorized;
5. **Silver Intelligence** — external confirmation and challenge context;
6. **Market Structure** — the authoritative confirmation or invalidation
   boundary.

## Read each card

The collapsed card answers:

- what is happening;
- why it matters;
- what it means for silver;
- the operational conclusion;
- what must confirm next;
- the primary risk;
- confidence and freshness.

Do not begin with raw metrics. Expand evidence only when validating a
conclusion or investigating a limitation.

## Authority labels

- **Production Approved**: permitted production evidence.
- **Validation**: validated but limited to approved influence.
- **Shadow Observation**: context only; cannot determine production bias.
- **Research**: internal explanation only.
- **Blocked**: no interpretation is authorized.
- **Unavailable**: evidence is absent; this is not neutral.

## Degraded states

- **Stale** means the observation cannot support a current conclusion.
- **Blocked** means a required data or validation gate is unresolved.
- **Unavailable** means the system has no eligible evidence.
- **Partial** limits the conclusion and confidence.

The operator must not mentally fill a missing section with the previous state.
Check the timestamp, reason codes, limitations, and required confirmation.

## Liquidity

Until a provider is connected, Liquidity Environment must say that
institutional order-book data is unavailable. It cannot describe resting
liquidity, absorption, iceberg activity, or institutional participation.

## Decision use

HEL-035 is decision support, not trade execution. Market Structure remains the
operational confirmation boundary. Shadow intelligence may support or
challenge the assessment but cannot create an entry, exit, alert, or score
change.
