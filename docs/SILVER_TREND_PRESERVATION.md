# XAGUSD trend-preservation evidence

The dashboard and Executive Briefing V2 consume the same generated operator copy. This is historical tolerance research; it does not route orders, publish stops, alter instrument scoring, change scheduling, or replace the live auction view.

## How to read it

- **Weekly**: Tuesday 09:00 ET through Friday close in an otherwise complete Monday–Friday archive week. **Rolling weekly**: Wednesday 09:00 ET through the fifth session (Tuesday of the following week). **Multi-day**: Wednesday through Friday. **Daily**: 09:00 ET through the session close. **Monthly**: 20 weekdays, with a fixed Wednesday anchor every four weeks. Missing grid slots or missing weekdays exclude the window. No holiday interpolation or provider splicing. Horizons are not pooled or assumed independent across cohorts.
- The historical tolerance p75 describes the largest **recovered** close-to-close retracement in each retrospectively healthy path. “Healthy” means the window closed by at least 0.25 lagged day ranges times the square root of holding sessions and retained at least 40% of favorable close excursion. Descriptive direction is assigned after the window ends. Percentiles omit paths without a positive recovered pullback. Those paths are still counted in the healthy denominator.
- Display requires at least 25 positive recovered paths and four distinct year blocks. Intervals resample whole archived years (300 fixed-seed draws). The intervals describe archive sensitivity; they are not a conditional forward probability. P95 is withheld below 100 recovered paths. The monthly result does not qualify.
- Replay uses only an observable hourly prefix; direction and eligibility never use the eventual extreme. The hypothetical fill is the next M5 open. Prefixes use the lagged median day range and existing signed efficiency classifier; the hour is in New York time. The 03:00 ET sensitivity is a London-clock proxy, not an independently verified broker session. A real week’s final high/low is not used as an entry. Prior-session and developing-week tags use two already closed bars; the reclaim tag uses only an extreme observed before entry.
- Development through Dec 2022, validation in 2023, and a separately reported 2024–25 **previously examined archive replay**. Windows straddling cutoffs are excluded from policy evaluation. The read-only builder does not change the earlier holdout or freeze any new operational threshold. Comparator: width one lagged day range; activation at half a lagged day range of favorable movement; modeled round-trip costs of one percent of that prior range. Additional sensitivity covers other widths, favorable activation and a three-percent modeled cost. No broker-specific fill or cost claim. A ratchet moves only after the bar closes; no intrabar sequence is fabricated.
- Drawdown overlap compares healthy paths with failures and reversals. Its AUC is the empirical probability that a deteriorating path has larger maximum close drawdown, counting ties as half; this retrospective rank comparison is not an operational classifier. Retained and giveback are reported for all simulated outcomes in each slice, not only the healthy survivors.
- Historical paths come from EV Trading Labs / Dukascopy bid candles. Recent HistData is separately evaluated on a comparable clock, not spliced or treated as a substitute. Live biquote is indicative OTC; coverage is not sufficient for a current volatility adjustment. Quotation tick, decimal precision and execution-broker point size are not interchangeable. Conditional point equivalents can be calculated only under an explicit assumed increment.

## Reproduce the reviewed projection

Run `python tools/build_silver_trend.py --db <existing private XAGUSD SQLite> --out data/research/xagusd-trend-preservation.json`. The database is opened read-only. The generated artifact contains aggregate results, not raw OHLC. The notification branch projects the operator copy with `tools/build-executive-research.mjs`; a SHA-256 in the generated module binds it to the dashboard artifact.

The executive-only projection is separately built by `python tools/build_silver_trend_executive.py --research data/research/xagusd-trend-preservation.json --db <existing private XAGUSD SQLite> --out data/research/xagusd-trend-executive.json`. It does not overwrite the original evidence. The dashboard cards and both briefing channels use the same data; the assumed point increment is $0.001/oz and conversion rounds half up. The execution-broker specification is not verified.

A separate nonoverlapping calendar-month check starts on each month's first weekday and requires the first 20 weekdays to be in that month and fully observed. Healthy recovered counts were 5 bullish / 6 bearish (EV full), 11 / 12 (EV core), and 0 / 0 (recent HistData core). These overlap the original schedule of 20-session windows, so the counts are not pooled. Neither alternate nor original full-session cohort qualifies. Weekly normalization compared recovered depth with the lagged median of completed day ranges on separate core-hour provider cohorts (EV fit: 21 bullish / 18 bearish; recent HistData: 1 / 0). These are below the frozen per-direction fit and recent coverage requirements; historical figures are not mechanically scaled and biquote is not used to fill historical gaps. Both audits are visible in Supporting Research & Validation.
