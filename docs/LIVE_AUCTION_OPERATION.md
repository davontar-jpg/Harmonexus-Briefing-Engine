## Evidence gates

The local repository uses an existing provider/resolution/start-keyed SQLite store; duplicates are ignored and revisions that conflict are rejected. The broker adapter consumes only closed, UTC-aligned five-minute midpoint bars with valid OHLC. It can derive 15-minute, one-hour and four-hour OHLC from complete contiguous bins. Calendar-day and week extrema use broker-session boundaries, NY DST and verified contiguous candles. A partial or stale feed cannot generate a full-session extreme. The canonical on-demand response covers at most 5,000 recent bars, so an operating weekly range baseline and remaining-expansion forecast are withheld. The short-window daily range percentile/completion comparison needs at least 15 normally scheduled, complete same-provider sessions; it is not a forecast. Price acceptance is two consecutive closed five-minute bars outside the relevant verified prior-session boundary, not an inference about resting orders or participation.

Weekly range and extrema, session range added, and a daily median are reported only when their own coverage gates pass. Session labels use Tokyo, London and New York local clocks; windows can overlap. The 20-minute freshness gate is applied again at rendering time. Without entitlement, the view and briefing retain historical weekday and retracement evidence; existing sample results and failed stop-promotion conclusions are unchanged. No new directional forecast, live trailing reference, broker-point conversion, or material-change alert is active. HEL-034 remains shadow-only: November runtime publication does not refresh its July observations, and a fresh synchronized XAGUSD control stream was not available to qualify a new shadow record.

FRED health is based on individual observation dates, not score refresh time. Weekend and recent scheduled publication lag are not reported as provider outages. The daily yield/VIX data remain published daily observations, not intraday pricing. Direct FRED probing did not establish newer usable observations; do not replace an existing verified series with a guessed value. Triggers and refresh cadence were not changed.
